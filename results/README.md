# Experimental Results and Artifacts

This directory contains the experimental logs, tables, metrics, and publication figures for the paper *"Who Saw It First? Zero-Day Propagation and Attribution Leakage in Federated Intrusion Detection"* (EuroS&P 2027).

In strict adherence to **ADR-0001**, all statistical summaries, 95% confidence intervals, and vector graphics are generated by the decoupled R analysis engine under `analysis/`.

## Directory Structure

- `figures/`: Publication vector PDFs (8 pt fonts, venue styling):
  - `fig1a_propagation.pdf`: Zero-day recall propagation trajectory over training rounds.
  - `fig1b_skew_vs_tau.pdf`: Impact of non-IID Dirichlet skew $\alpha$ on propagation delay $\tau_{0.80}$.
  - `fig2_pareto_frontier.pdf`: Speed–Leakage Pareto frontier ($\tau_{0.80}$ vs. Top-1 origin attribution accuracy across DP, Delay, and Quorum defenses).
- `tables/`: Publication-ready LaTeX tables:
  - `table3_attack_success.tex`: Origin attribution accuracy (Top-1, Top-3) and timing error (MAE) across Server (Unprotected), Server (SecAgg), and Curious Peer settings in Forensic and Surrogate modes.
  - `table_defense_tradeoff.tex`: Defense trade-offs comparing propagation speed, attribution leakage, timing error, and known attack $F_1$.
  - `table_sec5_5_robustness.tex`: Section 5.5 operational robustness under SOC label delay, unlabeled autoencoders, concurrent outbreaks, and concept drift.
- `metrics/`: Raw and aggregated experiment Parquet/CSV tables:
  - `table3_results.parquet`
  - `defense_pareto_results.parquet`
  - `sec5_5_robustness_results.parquet`
- `logs/`: End-to-end execution logs:
  - `smoke.log`
  - `phase3_table3.log`
  - `phase4_defenses.log`
  - `phase5_robustness.log`
